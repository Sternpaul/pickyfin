Checking the AI's work

Second opinion

A different model reviews every movie's tags, 40 at a time, and names only what looks wrong. Much quicker than describing. Flagged movies show up under Issues.

Web lookups with Exa

For films the AI doesn't know: a web search finds a real description (Wikipedia, Letterboxd, TMDB…), and the film is described again with it. Under one cent per film. Any movie can also be looked up by hand from its page under Movies. A key comes from dashboard.exa.ai.

Look up flagged films on their own

When the AI says it doesn't know a film, or something looks off, look it up and describe it again right away. Each film is looked up once.
